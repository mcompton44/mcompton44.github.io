## Day 22: Using GAIA Cone Search

Today I went through my folder of potential double-period lightcurve/sources and ran their RAs and DECs through the GAIA cone search to see if there were nearby
sources of similar brightness (based on mean G mag) that could be a cause of the apparent double periods. I made three folders, one for no nearby sources, one 
for nearby sources but with different (or dimmer) brightness levels, and one with nearby sources of similar (same whole number) or greater brightness. I was 
wondering what the specific cutoff of G mag difference should be for considering whether an alternate source would be close enough in brightness to interefere with 
the main source, so that is something I will look into more tomorrow. Tomorrow I also want to start looking at the Lomb-Scargle periodograms for some of my sources.
