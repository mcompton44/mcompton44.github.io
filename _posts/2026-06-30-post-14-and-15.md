Day 14 & 15: Looking for Double Periods and Sorting Eclipsing Binaries

Thursday and Friday were spent looking for more of the strange double period stars, and sorting our eclipsing binary folder based on what we got from the Liu
catalog. I learned more about the different typed of eclipsing binaries (EA, EB, EW), and as a group me, Sara, & Aidan were able to identify a previously missed
EW eclipsing binary. We also had a discussion about our 'weird TGLC' folder and established a system for creating subgroups within that folder, and thinking
of strategies to improve distorted/noisy lightcurves. Below I will put in some of the coolest double period lightcurves I found.
