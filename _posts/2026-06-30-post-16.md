## Day 16: Sorting "Weird TGLC" Folder and Trying to Improve Sawtooth Lightcurves

Today I worked on further categorizing our weird folder into blank, change x-axis, outlier spike/change y-axis, fainter than 12 Gmag, potential
heartbeat, sawtooth, and sector discrepancy. We did this in the hopes of improving these lightcurves in the future by identifying their issues 
so that they are more usable and easier to analyze. I attempted to get rid of the sawtooth shape in some of the graphs using a tutorial, but this 
did not prove very fruitful at all. The program essentially spits out the original lightcurve despite trying to remove the background noise responsible 
for the sawtooth shape. I am hoping TGLC will be helpful in getting more useful lightcurves from these ones.
