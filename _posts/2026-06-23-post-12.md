## Day 12: Almost Done with Sorting Lightcurves

Today I had something unexpected come up, and had a fews hours where I wasn't sorting, but I still got through a good amount of lightcurves, and I think 
they will all be sorted before the afternoon group meeting tomorrow. Today I realized that some of the lightcurves I thought were eclipsing binaries
were actually just due to offset spot distribution, and that's what was producing the cubic polynomial shape in some of the curves. I also talked to
Dr. G about some light curves that had two different periods going on, which created these bead shapes of smaller oscillations, and that was cool to realize that that was actually what was going on in the star system and it wasn't some sort of instrumental glitch that was causing those shapes.
