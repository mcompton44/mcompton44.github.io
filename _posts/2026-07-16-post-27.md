## Day 27: Finishing Up Potential Intefering Source Documentation and Creating More Periodograms

Today I worked on finishing up my catalog for the lightcurves whose original source also had sources withing 31.5 arcsec that could potentiall be contaminating the
lightcurve. This has been taking a very long time, but will hopefully be helpful in making the distinction between polluted lightcurves and more true double period
objects. I also worked on making periodograms for another 'no nearby source' object, and these had a really interesting pattern, and the rest of the code for the 
lomb-scargle periodogram tutorial produced other figures that made more sense than the other lightcurves I had tried in my previous post. Last post, the figures
had merely reproduced the original lightcurve marked with day counts that were vastly off, but for this object they actually produced the graphs that I think look 
how they are supposed to, and are another interpretation of the periodogram having to do with the phase and folding the data. In the periodogram, I thought it was
intriguing that it seems to show the harmonics in the signal, while the other periodograms for the first object I tried to make did not show these evenly spaced peaks.
I think this periodogram and subsequent graphs turned out better than the first one, which seemed to have some issues going on even after cleaning the data.
